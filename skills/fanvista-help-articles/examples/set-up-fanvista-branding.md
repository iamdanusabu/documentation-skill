FanVista is the ordering page your fans open on their phones to buy merchandise, food and drinks at your events. Before fans can use it, you tell FanVista how the page looks, how fans sign in, and where their orders go. You do all of this on one page in the Retail Console, called **Setup**.

This article takes you through that page from top to bottom. Allow about 10 minutes. You do this once for your venue. It applies to every event.

## What you will set up

| Section on the page | What you decide | What it means for your fans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Titles, colors and image | The text, brand colors and picture on your sign-in screen | The first thing they see when they open your ordering page |
| **Login Options** | Whether fans sign in with a mobile number, an email address, or either | How they get in. FanVista sends a one-time code, so there is no password to remember |
| **Fulfillment Location** | The store that prepares FanVista orders | The stock they can buy, and where your staff pack their order |
| **Delivery Method** | Pickup, in-seat delivery, or both, and your pickup times | The choices they are offered at checkout |
| **Customized URL** | Your FanVista web address | The link and QR codes they use to reach you |
| **Suite Management** | Whether suite owners, general fans, or both can order | Which sign-in choices appear |

## Before you start

- FanVista is activated on your account by retailcloud. If you do not see **FanVista** in the Retail Console, contact retailcloud Support.
- The store where your staff will pick and pack FanVista orders is already set up in the Retail Console.
- Your brand colors as hex codes. A hex code is a # followed by 6 letters and numbers, for example #032f94. Your designer or brand guide has these.
- A square image for your sign-in screen. It must be 1080 x 1080 pixels and no larger than 5 MB.
- The name you want in your web address. Choose carefully, because you cannot change it later.

## Step 1: Open the Setup page

1. Sign in to the Retail Console.
2. In the menu on the left, select **FanVista**. The menu opens to show the FanVista pages.
3. Select **Setup**.

   ![Retail Console menu with FanVista opened and Setup selected](images/set-up-fanvista-branding/01-fanvista-setup-menu.png)

The **Setup** page opens. On the left are the settings. On the right is a phone preview that shows your sign-in screen as fans will see it. The preview updates as you make changes, so you can check your work as you go.

## Step 2: Add your titles and colors

The titles are the words fans read on your sign-in screen. The colors are used for buttons and highlights across your ordering page.

1. Enter your text in **Title 1**, **Title 2** and **Title 3**. Use your venue or team name and a short welcome line, for example "Seamless in-seat ordering".
2. In **Primary Color**, enter the hex code of your main brand color. FanVista uses it for buttons, such as the **Continue** button on the sign-in screen.
3. In **Secondary Color**, enter the hex code of your second brand color.
4. Look at the phone preview. Check that each title appears where you expect and that the text is easy to read against your colors.

   ![Primary Color and Secondary Color fields, with the Login Screen Background Image upload below](images/set-up-fanvista-branding/02-colors-and-background-image.png)

:::(Info) (Tip)
Keep titles short. Fans read them on a phone, often in a crowd. A venue name and one line of welcome is enough.
:::

## Step 3: Add your sign-in screen image

The background image fills the sign-in screen behind your titles. A photo of your venue, your team or your event works well.

1. Under **Login Screen Background Image**, select **Upload Image**.
2. Choose your image. It must be 1080 x 1080 pixels and no larger than 5 MB.
3. Check the phone preview. Make sure your titles can still be read on top of the image.

To remove the image and start again, select the trash icon next to **Upload Image**.

## Step 4: Choose how fans sign in

Fans do not create a password. They enter a mobile number or an email address, FanVista sends them a one-time code, and they enter the code to sign in. You choose which of the two they can use.

1. Scroll to **Login Options**.
2. Turn on **Enable Mobile Number Login** to let fans sign in with a mobile number. FanVista sends the code by text message.
3. Turn on **Enable Email Login** to let fans sign in with an email address. FanVista sends the code to their inbox.

   ![Login Options with Enable Mobile Number Login and Enable Email Login turned on](images/set-up-fanvista-branding/03-login-options.png)

| Mobile number | Email | What fans can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On | On | Sign in with either. This is how FanVista starts, and it suits most venues |
| On | Off | Sign in with a mobile number only |
| Off | On | Sign in with an email address only |
| Off | Off | Not allowed. At least 1 must stay turned on |

Your choice applies everywhere fans sign in, including suite owners.

## Step 5: Choose your fulfillment location

The fulfillment location is the store where your staff pick and pack FanVista orders. This is the most important setting on the page, because FanVista uses this one store for 3 things:

- **Stock.** Fans can order only what this store has in stock.
- **Sales.** Each order is recorded as a sale in this store, and its stock goes down.
- **Order dockets.** The docket that tells your staff a new order has arrived prints at this store.

1. Scroll to **Fulfillment Location**.
2. Select your store from **Store**. This field is required.

:::(Warning) (Warning)
Select the store where orders are physically prepared. If you select a different store, fans see the wrong stock, sales are recorded in the wrong place, and dockets print where nobody is waiting for them.
:::

## Step 6: Choose how fans receive their orders

You can offer pickup, in-seat delivery, or both. Fans choose between the methods you offer when they check out.

- **Pickup.** The fan collects the order from a pickup point at a time they choose.
- **In-Seat.** Your staff bring the order to the fan's seat section.

1. Scroll to **Delivery Method**.
2. Select **Pickup**, **In-Seat**, or both.
3. If you selected **Pickup**, set your pickup times under **Configure Pickup Time**:
   - In **From** and **To**, enter the start and end of a time window, for example 12:00 PM to 12:30 PM.
   - To add another window, select the plus icon below the first one.
   - To remove a window, select the trash icon next to it.
4. If you selected **In-Seat**, set up the seat sections fans can choose from.

   ![Delivery Method with Pickup selected and a pickup time window under Configure Pickup Time](images/set-up-fanvista-branding/04-delivery-method-and-pickup-time.png)

At checkout, a fan who chooses pickup selects one of your time windows. Short windows spread your orders evenly, so your staff are not asked to hand over every order at once. For example, 4 windows of 30 minutes each work better than 1 window of 2 hours.

:::(Info) (Note)
The pickup times you set here are your defaults for every event. If one event needs different times, you can set them on that event when you create it. Fans only ever see windows that have not yet passed.
:::

## Step 7: Create your web address

Your web address is where fans find your ordering page. It is also built into every QR code you print.

1. Scroll to **Customized URL**.
2. Enter a name, with no spaces. Most venues use the name of their business, for example yourvenue.
3. Read the full address shown next to the field. Your name is followed by .fanvista.app, so the example becomes yourvenue.fanvista.app.

:::(Warning) (Warning)
You cannot change this later. Check the spelling before you save. Your QR codes, signs and links all depend on this address.
:::

## Step 8: Choose who can order

FanVista can serve 2 kinds of customer. Suite owners have a booked suite and order for it. Fan guests are everyone else. Turn on the ones you serve.

1. Scroll to **Suite Management**.
2. Turn on **Enable Suite Management** if you take orders from suites. This shows the suite features in the Retail Console: **Bookings** in the FanVista menu, the suite tab in **Setup**, and the suite options in the QR Code Generator.
3. Turn on **Enable Fan Guest Login** to let fans without a suite sign in and order.

   ![Suite Management with Enable Suite Management and Enable Fan Guest Login turned on](images/set-up-fanvista-branding/05-suite-management.png)

| Your venue | Enable Suite Management | Enable Fan Guest Login |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sells to general fans only | Off | On |
| Sells to suite owners only | On | Off |
| Sells to both | On | On |

If you do not have suites, leave **Enable Suite Management** turned off. Your menu and your QR code options stay shorter, and fans are not shown a suite sign-in they cannot use.

## Step 9: Save and check your work

1. Save your changes.
2. On your phone, open your web address, for example yourvenue.fanvista.app.
3. Check the sign-in screen. You see your titles, colors and image, and only the sign-in options you turned on.

## What happens next

Your ordering page now has its look, its address and its sign-in options. Fans cannot order yet, because FanVista still needs to know how they pay and what you sell. Next, see *Connect payments for FanVista*. After that, see *Set up customer notifications for FanVista*.

## Good to know

- You can return to **Setup** at any time to change your titles, colors, image, sign-in options, pickup times and suite options. The only thing you cannot change is your web address.
- Orders from fan guests always use the store under **Fulfillment Location**. A suite can have its own store. A suite without one uses the fulfillment location.
- When **Pickup** is selected, you must add at least 1 pickup time window before you can save.
- When **Enable Suite Management** is turned off, suite sign-in, suite bookings and suite QR codes are hidden everywhere in FanVista.

## Common questions

### Can I use my own domain name instead of fanvista.app?
No. Every FanVista ordering page uses an address that ends in .fanvista.app. You choose the name that comes before it.

### Do fans need to download an app?
No. FanVista opens in the web browser on their phone. They reach it by scanning a QR code or opening your link.

### Do fans need a password?
No. They enter their mobile number or email address and receive a one-time code each time they sign in.

### Can I offer pickup at one event and in-seat delivery at another?
The delivery methods you select here are the ones FanVista can offer. Select both here, then set the pickup locations and times for each event when you create it.

### Which store should I choose if I have more than one?
Choose the store whose stock you want to sell through FanVista and where your staff will pack the orders. If orders are packed at a merchandise stand inside the venue, choose that stand's store.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Please configure the pickup time before saving." | **Pickup** is selected, but there is no pickup time window | Enter a window in **From** and **To**, then save again |
| Your image does not upload | The file is larger than 5 MB, or it is not 1080 x 1080 pixels | Resize the image, then select **Upload Image** again |
| Your titles are hard to read in the preview | The text and the image behind it are too close in color | Use a darker or simpler image, or shorten your titles |
| A fan does not receive a sign-in code by text message | The mobile number was entered incorrectly, or the message is delayed | Ask the fan to check the number and try again, or to sign in with email. Keep **Enable Email Login** turned on so fans always have a second way in |
| No docket prints, or stock does not go down after an order | A different store is selected under **Fulfillment Location** | Select the store where orders are prepared. See *Order dockets or receipts are not printing* |
| Fans see "Authentication Failed. Please Try Again" when they open your address | Your address is not ready for sign-in yet | Contact retailcloud Support |
| You do not see **Bookings** in the FanVista menu | **Enable Suite Management** is turned off | Turn it on, then save |
