Use OrderUp to pick, pack and hand over FanVista merchandise orders on game day. These steps take 1 order from the printed docket to the fan.

## When you would do this

On event day, each time a docket prints at your counter.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to OrderUp on your device.
- A receipt printer is paired with your device. If you have not done this yet, see *Pair a receipt printer in OrderUp*.

## Steps

1. A docket prints at your counter. Take it to the stock area.
2. Pick the items listed on the docket.
3. Scan the QR code on the docket with OrderUp. The order opens. [VERIFY: the name of the scan control in OrderUp]
4. Pack the items in a bag. OrderUp prints 2 receipts on your receipt printer. [VERIFY: whether the receipts print automatically or staff select a control to print them]
5. Put the customer receipt inside the bag.
6. Put the delivery receipt on the outside of the bag, with its QR code showing.

   [SCREENSHOT: a packed bag with the delivery receipt and its QR code visible on the outside]

7. Take the bag to the staging area.
8. Select **Ready**. The fan receives a message that the order is ready. [VERIFY: how staff mark an order Ready and Delivered in OrderUp, for example a button on the order]
9. The fan shows a QR code at the counter. Scan it. The order opens.
10. Scan the QR code on the bag's delivery receipt. This confirms that the bag matches the order.
11. Hand the bag to the fan.
12. Select **Delivered**.

For an in-seat delivery order, follow steps 1 to 6. Then take the bag to the seat section printed on the docket. [VERIFY: for in-seat delivery, whether staff select Ready, whether a QR code is scanned at the seat, and when staff select Delivered]

## What happens next

To confirm it worked, check that the order is marked **Delivered** in OrderUp.

Next, to pick several orders in 1 trip, see *Pick several orders at once with a batch picklist*.

## Rules to know

- OrderUp handles merchandise. Food and beverage items go to the kitchen.
- Only admin roles can use **Mark All Ready**. All other staff select **Ready** on 1 order at a time. See *Who can mark all orders ready*.
- An order with an **Updated** badge changed after it was placed. Its docket is headed **ORDER UPDATED**. See *Handle an order that changed after it was placed*.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No docket prints | The docket printer is offline or not set up for your counter | Tell your manager. |
| No receipts print when you pack | The receipt printer is not paired with your device | See *Pair a receipt printer in OrderUp* |
| The fan has no QR code | The ready message did not include one | Scan the QR code or barcode in the fan's order history in FanVista |
| A food or beverage item appears in OrderUp | The item is set up as merchandise | Tell your manager. |

For other printing problems, see *Order dockets or receipts are not printing*.
