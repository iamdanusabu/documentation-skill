Use FanVista to order merchandise or food and drinks from your phone at an event. There is nothing to download.

## Steps

1. Scan the venue's QR code with your phone camera, or open the venue's link.
2. Enter your mobile number or email to sign in. You see only the sign-in options the venue offers.
3. Optional: tap your language. FanVista offers English, German, Spanish and French.
4. Tap your event. You see only events that are open for ordering.
5. Tap an item on the menu. Tap a size and color if the item has them.
6. Tap the button that adds the item to your cart.
7. Open your cart and continue to checkout. Keep the page open until you pay, because your cart is not saved if you close it.
8. Tap **Pickup** or **In-Seat Delivery**. You see only the options the venue offers.
   - For **Pickup**, tap a pickup location and a time window.
   - For **In-Seat Delivery**, enter your seat section.
9. Optional: tap a tip amount, if the venue offers tipping.
10. Tap a way to pay: a card, a saved card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. A confirmation arrives by email or text.
11. A message arrives when your order is ready. It has a QR code. Show the QR code at your pickup location.


For **In-Seat Delivery**, stay at your seat. Venue staff bring your order to your seat section.

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot see your event. Events appear only while ordering is open. **Ordering closed** means ordering has ended for today.
- An item shows **Sold Out**. Tap a different item.
- Your cart is empty. The cart is not saved if you close the page or lose your connection. Add your items again.
- Apple Pay or Google Pay is not offered. Apple Pay works on an iPhone, or in Safari on a Mac. Google Pay works on an Android phone, or in Chrome. Pay by card on any other device.
- You have no QR code at the pickup location. Open your order history in FanVista. Each order there has a QR code that staff can scan.
- Something else is wrong. Ask a member of venue staff.
